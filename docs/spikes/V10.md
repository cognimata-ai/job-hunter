# V10: JSearch `job_uid` across days, and the day-old cursor

Status: **complete (2026-10-19). `job_uid` is stable across days: 7 of page 1's 10 `job_uid`s came back
unchanged on day 2. The 24-hour-old cursor still returned page 2, so `CURSOR_REUSE_TTL` is 24 h.** V9 phase 3
(the 24-hour cursor reuse) is this spike's request (7); `V9.md` records the TTL it set.

## Assumption

The same JSearch query sent on two days returns the ads that are on both days under identical `job_uid`s
(spec §22 V10; the `job_uid` is the Google `htidocid`). Acceptance (plan Task 6 step 4.3): at least 3 `job_uid`s of
`search_v2_page1_day2` are identical with `search_v2_page1`; fewer means the identity is unstable. That case
would be analysed and resolved before M1 acceptance. The same phase decides `jsearch.CURSOR_REUSE_TTL` by the
longest reuse that was *observed* to work, never by extrapolation:
- `timedelta(hours=24)` when request (7) returns a page that overlaps `search_v2_page2`;
- `timedelta(hours=2)` when only V9 phase 2's request (5) did;
- `timedelta(0)` otherwise.

## Method

The recorder ran `scripts/spikes/record_sources.py jsearch --phase 3`. It is budgeted and counts each request
before its send. It refuses this phase until 24 h after the end of phase 1 (2026-10-19T20:50:21Z) and never
runs a phase twice. The phase ran on 2026-10-19 from 20:52:11.898Z to 20:52:27.314Z and sent two requests:

6. `GET /search-v2` with exactly the parameters of V9 request (2): `query="software engineer in Berlin, Germany"`,
   `country=de`, `num_pages=1` and `date_posted=week`. Case `search_v2_page1_day2`, sent 24.03 h after (2).
7. The same request plus the cursor of `search_v2_page1` (V9 request (2)), the value that V9's (4) and (5) also
   sent. Case `search_v2_page2_day2`. **The cursor was 24.04 h old** (counted from page 1's `fetched_at`,
   2026-10-18T20:50:15Z). It was sent 1.5 s after (6) answered.

JSearch budget: 5 → **7 of 8**. The 8th request is the spare for a transport failure. It was not used, and since
the recorder never re-runs a phase it cannot buy a new case.

The comparison sets of `job_uid` were taken from the raw recordings. The scrubber keeps `job_uid` unchanged, so
the committed fixtures give the same counts.

| Request | Case | Sent (UTC) | Status | Jobs | `data.cursor` | `x-ratelimit-requests-remaining` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| (7) | `search_v2_page2_day2` | 20:52:22.329 | 200 | 8 | set | 193 |

- **Billing: 1 credit per page, as in V9.**
  - Remaining credits went 195 (after V9 phase 2) → 194 → 193. The free-plans hard-limit counter went
    499995 → 499994 → 499993.
  - The reset was 2,376,124 s at (6), which still puts the window end at 2026-11-16T08:54:24Z. No other call
    was billed between phase 2 and phase 3.
- **V10, identity: 7 of page 1's 10 `job_uid`s are on `search_v2_page1_day2`, so at least 3 are identical.**
  - Five of the 7 kept their position. The other two moved down by one.
  - On all 7, the title, employer, publisher, `job_location`, `job_posted_at_timestamp` and `job_description`
    are identical across the two days.
  - `job_apply_link` is identical except on one LinkedIn ad. Its day-1 link carried tracking parameters
    (`position`, `pageNum`, `refId`, `trackingId`) and its day-2 link has none. The path is the same, and
    `canonical_url` and `keys_from_link` give equal results for both links.
  - `job_id` differs on all 7. The blob after `<job_uid>:` changes, as V9 saw within one day. `job_id` is not an
    identity; the adapter reads `job_uid` only.
- **The ads not shared are not renamed ones.**
  - The other 3 ads of day 1's page 1, posted 2026-10-15 and 2026-10-16, are on neither day-2 page.
  - The 3 new ads of day 2's page 1 were posted 2026-10-13 to 2026-10-16, so they already existed on day 1.
    None of them matches a day-1 ad by title and employer, or by apply link.
  - No ad came back under a new `job_uid`.
  - One day-2 ad is a cross-publisher duplicate: the same vitaservices ad via Stepstone (on both days) and via
    Trabajo.org (day 2 only) has two `job_uid`s. That is the probable-duplicate path, not an unstable identity.
  - Over both pages, 13 of the 21 `job_uid`s seen on day 1 (`search_v2_page1`, `search_v2_page2` and
    `search_v2_page2_reused`) are among day 2's 18.
- **Cursor reuse after 24 h, request (7): 200 with 8 jobs.** 6 of them are `job_uid`s of `search_v2_page2` (5 are
  `job_uid`s of `search_v2_page2_reused`), and none is on `search_v2_page1` or `search_v2_page1_day2`.
  - The day-old cursor still addresses page 2 of the same query. The results have moved on: 2 ads are new.
  - The page has its own cursor, which differs from page 2's. The request cost 1 credit.
  - The cursor is opaque (base64url of a binary message). Day 2's page 1 handed out a different cursor value
    from day 1's for the same query.
- **Buckets:** for `week` on day 2, page 1's ages are 92.9–164.9 h and page 2's are 116.9–188.9 h. Both stay
  within the day-truncated bound of V9 (≤ 192 h). Phase 3 has no `date_posted=today` request, so V9's 48-hour
  check of `today` is still vacuous: its only recording is an empty page.
- **By-product: structured locations.** `job_city`, `job_country`, `job_latitude` and `job_longitude` are set on
  5 of the 18 day-2 items (`Berlin`, `DE`; via Learn4Good ×3, BeBee and Veralto Global Jobs). V9 saw them null
  on all 19 day-1 items. `job_location` is present on every item as before, so the adapter's location parse
  (`parse_jsearch_location` of `job_location`) is unaffected. The one new location string,
  `Berlin • über Arbeitnow`, is labelled `DE`, `city` in `tests/contract/fixtures/location_labels.tsv`.

## Decision

- **V10 is accepted:** `job_uid` is stable across days. 7 identical `job_uid`s, at least 3 as required, all with
  unchanged content, and no ad came back under a new `job_uid`. `native_id = job_uid` and the
  `htidocid:<job_uid>` key stay as they are. There is no identity instability for Task 25's gate to resolve.
- **`jsearch.CURSOR_REUSE_TTL = timedelta(hours=24)`.** Request (7) returned a page that overlaps
  `search_v2_page2` with a 24.04 h old cursor. 24 h is the longest interval at which reuse was observed to
  work. No longer interval was tested, so none is assumed.
- The day-2 recordings are committed as `jsearch/search_v2_page1_day2` and `jsearch/search_v2_page2_day2`. They
  are not routed in `sources.fixtures.ROUTES`, because no test needs them.
