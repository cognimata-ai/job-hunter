# V3: SerpApi Google Jobs with `hl=en`

## Assumption

A SerpApi `google_jobs` search with `hl=en` yields `detected_extensions` and the same `htidocid` that JSearch
(and `hl=de`) report for the same ad, so `SERPAPI_HL = "en"` keeps identity across the two paid sources (spec
§5.3.2, §22 V3). Acceptance (§22): at least 3 overlapping ads with an identical `htidocid`; otherwise
`SERPAPI_HL = "de"` stays, and the bilingual parsers are required anyway.

## Method

`scripts/spikes/record_sources.py serpapi` on 2026-10-18 (plan Task 6 step 4.4), 22 s after the JSearch page 1 of
V9: a free `account.json` read (`account_before`), one search `engine=google_jobs`,
`q=software engineer Berlin`, `gl=de`, `hl=en` (`google_jobs_hl_en`, 1 unit), and a second free `account.json`
read 130 s later (`account_after`; SerpApi's counters lag a search, `SERPAPI_USAGE_LAG` 120 s). Each result's
`job_id` was base64-decoded and its `htidocid` compared with the `job_uid`s of `jsearch/search_v2_page1`
(`software engineer in Berlin, Germany`, `country=de`, `week`).

## Observed result

- **One unit spent, the account reads free:** `plan_searches_left` 248 → 247, `this_month_usage` 2 → 3,
  `this_hour_searches` 0 → 1 across the search (two searches had been made this month before the spike).
  `searches_per_month` 250, `account_rate_limit_per_hour` 250, `plan_renewal_date` 2026-11-08, plan `free`.
  The account body also carries the account e-mail and the API key; the recorder replaced the key before writing
  and the scrubber pseudonymises both (`api_key` and `account_id` are `token` fields).
- **`htidocid`:** 10 results, each `job_id` a base64 JSON object with `job_title`, `company_name`, `htidocid`,
  `hl`, `gl`, `fc`, `fc_id`, `fcv`. **3 of the 10 `htidocid`s are identical with `job_uid`s of JSearch page 1**
  (2 more match JSearch page 2).
- **`detected_extensions`: absent on all 10 results.** Every result has `extensions` as free text in English
  (`["2 days ago", "Full-time"]`), which the bilingual `parse_extensions` reads. The response has
  `serpapi_pagination.next_page_token` (a continuation) and `filters`.
- The primary-link rule has its input: `via` and `apply_options[].title` are present on every result.

## Decision

**`SERPAPI_HL = "en"`.** The §22 acceptance criterion holds: 3 identical `htidocid`s, so `hl=en` keeps the
identity shared with JSearch. The decision follows the spec (§22's acceptance column, and §5.3.2: `de` stays only
if the `htidocid` differs), and it is the outcome the Task 9 contract test computes from this recording
(`"en" if overlap >= 3`).

The other half of the assumption does not hold: `hl=en` gave no `detected_extensions`. The Task 6 brief made
both halves the condition for `en`. **Controller ruling T6-a (2026-10-18): `SERPAPI_HL = "en"` stands.** Spec
§22's acceptance is ≥ 3 identical `htidocid`, and the brief's extra `detected_extensions` condition is not in
the spec. The bilingual parsers stay required, since `extensions` is the only date and schedule source in this
recording. `google_jobs_hl_en` is the contract fixture.
