# V14: the prompt cache across models, schemas and the structured-output framing

## Assumption

Spec §22 V14 (M2 Deviation 9; M3 Task 26, Rulings T26-a and T26-d): the score nodes share one cached prefix,
the rubric block and the profile block up to the one cache marker on `system[1]` (`render.score_prompt`, the
three-block layout). Four questions follow:
- Does the second of two identical calls inside the 5-minute TTL read the prefix? It should read at least
  4,000 tokens (V5's acceptance).
- Does a critic call on `claude-opus-5-5` read the entry a generator call on `claude-sonnet-5-5` wrote for the
  same prefix? It should not, because the model differs.
- A3 G7: are the structured-output framing tokens counted in the cached part or in `input_tokens`?
- A3 G6: do two schemas on one model and effort share a cache entry?

The first three are measured in V5's paid run (`docs/spikes/V5.md`, M3 section). The optional `v14` pair
answers G6, and the brief sends it only if V5 leaves G6 open (Ruling T26-a). V5 answered G6 by inference only,
so `v14` ran (Ruling T26-d).

## Method

- **V5's pairs.** 5 generator pairs on Sonnet and 5 critic pairs on Opus, all at effort `medium`, through the
  gateway, as `docs/spikes/V5.md` describes. Each pair has its own synthetic prefix. The generator and the
  critic of pair `k` share its prefix bytes, and the two calls of a pair are the same request, the second
  sent as soon as the first settled. Free `count_tokens` requests split the first generator and critic
  requests into their components, for the G7 arithmetic.
- **The `v14` pair.** The spike `v14` (`scripts/spikes/v5_stream_timing.py`, run as `record_anthropic.py
  --spike v14 --dry-run --through-gateway`, then `--live --through-gateway`, on 2026-10-29 at 02:14 UTC) sends
  one pair on a sixth synthetic prefix through the gateway, as V5 does:
  - `g6`: the generator, with schema C;
  - `x6`: the critic prompt and the critic schema.

  Both calls run on `claude-sonnet-5-5` at effort `medium`, and `x6` went as soon as `g6` settled. The two
  calls' prefix (the rubric block and the profile block up to the one cache marker) is byte for byte the same;
  the role template, the user turn and the schema differ. `SPIKE_CALLS["v14"] = 2` allowed one attempt each,
  under the $10.00 rule. The dry run checked both payloads offline (worst $0.262791).

## Observed result

**Identical pairs (V5).** Every first call of a pair wrote the prefix and read nothing. Every second call read
exactly what the first wrote (8,104 to 8,792 tokens, 10 of 10) and wrote nothing.

**Across models (V5).** The Opus critic never read the Sonnet generator's entry for the same prefix: each
`c<k>a` wrote its own entry.

**G7 (V5's arithmetic).** The cached part of each first call is the prefix plus the schema plus the framing,
to within 11 tokens:
- generator: 7,423 + 737 + 473 + 11 = 8,644;
- critic: 7,423 + 424 + 310 + 11 = 8,168.

The uncached `input_tokens` are the components after the marker, less the same 11: 1,792 and 3,494. In every
pair, the generator's entry is 476 tokens larger than the critic's. That is the schema difference (313) plus
the framing difference (163).

**The `v14` pair.** Both calls were sent once and answered HTTP 200 `end_turn` at the 6,000 stage.

- **`x6` read nothing.** It was sent right after `g6`, on the same model, effort and prefix. It wrote its own
  entry (8,218 tokens) and read 0. In V5, a pair's identical second call read its first call's entry in full
  every time.
- **The two entries differ by 476 tokens**, as in every V5 pair (313 for the schema, 163 for its framing). In
  V5 the two roles ran on two models; here they run on one. So the 476 tokens are the schema and its framing,
  not a model difference, and G7's finding holds on one model.
- **Neither call thought** (0 thinking tokens, no thinking block): the critic prompt on Sonnet behaves like the
  generator on Sonnet. No attempt was cut at `max_tokens` (0 of 2).
- **The analysis's `accepted` flag is false for this run** (`second_of_pair_reads_cache`). That criterion is
  V5's check for identical pairs, and a read of 0 is the observation G6 asks for. No acceptance of V5 depends
  on it.

## Decision

- **The prefix cache works through the gateway.** The first call of a prefix writes one entry, and the next
  identical call inside the TTL reads all of it.
- **Another model never reads the entry.** The critic on Opus writes its own.
- **G7 is answered: the framing is in the cached part.** The schema and its framing are counted in
  `cache_creation_input_tokens` on a cold call and in `cache_read_input_tokens` on a warm one, not in
  `input_tokens`. A cost estimate that prices the prefix at the cache rates must therefore include them: the
  generator's cached part is 8,580 to 8,792 tokens, not the 7,423 of the rubric and the profile.
- **G6 is answered, by observation: two schemas on one model and effort do not share a cache entry.** The
  schema and its framing are inside the cached prefix (G7), so a call with another schema writes its own entry,
  even when the rubric and profile bytes are identical. A cost estimate must count one cache write per prefix
  for each (model, schema) that uses it, not one per prefix.
- **Recordings.** These are committed with their sidecars:
  - `generate/claude-sonnet-5-5/7d5369246e82.g6.sse`;
  - `critic/claude-sonnet-5-5/cf6a3209541a.x6.sse`.

  They are case-named, so the fake serves neither. Neither stream had thinking to strip. The PII gate passes.
  V5's recordings are listed in `docs/spikes/V5.md`.
