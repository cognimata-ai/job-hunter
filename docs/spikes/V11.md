# V11: a LangGraph `TimeoutPolicy` cancels an in-flight streaming gateway call

## Assumption

Spec §22 V11 and §10.4: when a node's `TimeoutPolicy` fires, LangGraph cancels the node's in-flight streaming
gateway call, and the gateway settles that try `ambiguous` (it keeps its whole reservation). The success
criteria are that the cancellation lands within 1 s of the deadline, that the ledger entry is `ambiguous`, and
that no task is left behind.

## Method

The test is `tests/integration/test_gateway_timeout_policy.py::test_v11_timeout_policy_cancels_the_stream_and_the_try_settles_ambiguous`.
It runs on the digest-pinned PostgreSQL container, with no paid or networked call:

- A one-node `StateGraph` (langgraph 1.2.15) whose node calls `LLMGateway.call` for `GATEWAY_NODE`: an
  operation-owned `extract_profile` call with the test schema, a 900 s call deadline and
  `TimeoutPolicy(run_timeout=2)`.
- The gateway uses the recorded-SSE fake with the production 120 s read timeout, on the scenario
  `stall_after_first_event`. The fake sends the response headers and `message_start`, then nothing. So the
  read timeout cannot fire, and neither can the call's own 900 s deadline; only the policy can end the try.
- The test snapshots `asyncio.all_tasks()` before `graph.ainvoke`, and expects `NodeTimeoutError`. It then
  waits for exactly the tasks the call left (bounded at 5 s), and measures two moments from the start:
  - when `NodeTimeoutError` was raised;
  - when the cancelled call had finished, its settlement committed.

The figures below come from five runs of the same test on 2026-10-21, through a temporary copy that printed the
timings, the number of tasks left and their names. They contain no content.

## Observed result

| Run | `NodeTimeoutError` raised after | The cancelled call settled after | Tasks left when raised | Tasks left afterwards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2.005 s | 2.019 s | 3 | 0 |
| 2 | 2.004 s | 2.016 s | 3 | 0 |
| 3 | 2.004 s | 2.016 s | 3 | 0 |
| 4 | 2.005 s | 2.016 s | 3 | 0 |
| 5 | 2.004 s | 2.018 s | 3 | 0 |

- **The cancellation lands at the deadline.** `NodeTimeoutError` is raised 4 to 5 ms after the 2 s
  `run_timeout`. The cancelled gateway call has settled and ended at most 19 ms after it, well within the 1 s
  criterion.
- **The try is `ambiguous`.** The ledger entry is `ambiguous` with no `settled_usd` and no evidence. Its
  `llm_calls` row is `ambiguous`, with `error_code='cancelled'`, `cost_usd` equal to the reservation and
  `first_event_at` set, because the response had begun. The counters still equal the ledger. Exactly one
  request reached the fake.
- **No task is left behind**, but LangGraph does not wait for the cancelled node. `_arun_with_timeout`
  (`langgraph/pregel/_retry.py`) cancels the node's task, adds a done-callback that marks its exception as
  retrieved, and raises `NodeTimeoutError` at once.
  - The gateway's `ambiguous` settlement therefore runs in that cancelled task, just after the caller has seen
    the error.
  - Three tasks were still alive at that moment, by their coroutines: LangGraph's node runner
    (`_arun_with_timeout.<locals>.run`), the node's `RunnableCallable.ainvoke`, and the `RunnableParallel.ainvoke`
    of langchain's structured-output chain (`RunnableMap(raw=llm) | parser`).
  - All three ended within the next 15 ms. None was left afterwards.
- **The `CancelledError` travels cleanly through langchain.** It reaches the gateway's `ainvoke`, and the SDK
  stream's read is interrupted. The gateway settles the try in its own transaction and re-raises. No langchain
  layer swallows or converts it.

## Decision

- **V11 holds.** The gateway settles a cancelled try `ambiguous` in the cancelled task itself, in its own
  transaction, and re-raises the `CancelledError` (the `cancelled` row of the try table in `llm/gateway.py`).
- **A caller must not assume the try is settled when it sees `NodeTimeoutError`.** The settlement follows
  within milliseconds, but after the caller sees the error. If the process dies in between, or a second
  cancellation interrupts the settlement, the entry stays `dispatched`. `llm_ledger_unsettled` then marks it
  `ambiguous` after `max(15 min, deadline_s + 5 min)` (plan Deviation 24), so no outcome loses the charge.
- **The per-call deadline.** Plan Deviation 28 compiles the LLM nodes with `TimeoutPolicy(run_timeout=1800 +
  60)` and enforces the active `deadline_s` per call. The gateway bounds each try's stream by what is left of
  `CallContext.deadline` (`asyncio.timeout`). When that bound fires, the try settles `ambiguous` with
  `error_code='deadline'` and the call raises `DeadlineExhausted`, the same `transient_error` outcome as the
  policy (§10.4). This is pinned by `tests/integration/test_gateway.py::test_the_deadline_ends_a_try_in_flight_as_ambiguous`.
