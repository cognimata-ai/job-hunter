# V17: gazetteer coverage of real location strings

## Assumption

The location parser over the offline GeoNames gazetteer (§8.12) resolves at least 95% of the German city
strings that real sources send, and never calls a German location "outside Germany" (spec §22 V17).

## Method

`tests/contract/test_v17_gazetteer_coverage.py` over the scrubbed fixtures of Task 6 (2026-10-18; Task 6b added
Arbeitnow `page2` and JSearch `search_v2_page2_reused`). Every distinct
location string of the fixture bodies is collected: JSearch `job_location` (parsed with
`parse_jsearch_location`; `job_city` and `job_country` are null in every recording), SerpApi
`jobs_results[].location`, Adzuna `location.display_name`, Arbeitnow `location`, Greenhouse `location.name` and
`offices[].name`, Lever `categories.location`/`allLocations[]`, Ashby `location`, `secondaryLocations[].location`
and `address.postalAddress.addressLocality`, Personio `<office>`, Recruitee `location` and `city`,
SmartRecruiters `location.fullLocation` and `location.city`. That is 172 strings (159 in Task 6a; Task 6b added
12 Arbeitnow strings of `page2` and one JSearch string).

Each string was labelled by hand in `tests/contract/fixtures/location_labels.tsv` while the fixtures were reviewed:

- `country` is the ISO code, or `-` for remote, country-wide or unknown;
- `kind` is `city` 115, `multi` 4, `country_wide` 36, `remote` 2, `unknown` 15 (Task 6a: 104, 3, 35, 2, 15).

The test checks four things against those labels:

- **Resolved German:** among the strings labelled German `city`/`multi` (the denominator is the label), the share
  that parses as `city`/`multi` with no unresolved location.
- **False foreign:** a German string parsed `positively_non_de`.
- **False German:** a foreign string that resolves to German candidates only.
- **Kinds:** every `remote` and `country_wide` label is parsed with that kind.

A label is never edited to fit the parser.

## Observed result

| | Parser of Task 4 (commit 32007e8), 159 strings | After the fixes below, 159 strings (6a) | After the fixes, all 172 strings (6b) |
|---|---|---|---|
| German city strings resolved | **23 / 40 = 57.5%** | **40 / 40 = 100%** | **51 / 52 = 98.1%** |
| False "outside Germany" | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| False German | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| `remote` / `country_wide` kind mismatches | 33 | 0 | 0 |
| Foreign strings parsed positively non-German | 15 of 67 | 28 of 67 | 28 of 67 |

The one German string of 6b left unresolved is Arbeitnow's `Munich, Berlin, Germany`, labelled `multi`: a list
of two cities, which stays unresolved by design (below) and passes every filter.
No parser change was made for the 6b strings.

The failures of the Task 4 parser had three causes:

- **Comma qualifiers left unresolved by design** ("a comma separates a list as often as it qualifies a name"):
  - Adzuna's `<district>, <city>`: `Kreuzberg, Berlin`, `Mariendorf, Berlin`, `Adlershof, Berlin`,
    `Friedrichshain, Berlin`, `Wedding, Berlin`;
  - `<city>, <state>`: `Potsdam, Brandenburg`, `Munich, Bavaria`, `Eschborn, Hesse`, `Düsseldorf, North
    Rhine-Westphalia`;
  - Arbeitnow's `<city>, <state>, <country>`: `Detmold, Nordrhein-Westfalen, Deutschland`, `München, Bayern,
    Deutschland`, `Munich, Bayern, Germany`, `Berlin, Berlin, Deutschland` and the rest; abroad, Recruitee's
    and SmartRecruiters' `Amsterdam, Noord-Holland, Netherlands` and `Barcelona, , Spain`.
- **Ashby's site names** `Berlin Office` and `London Office`.
- **Markers and countries:** Arbeitnow's `Remote job` was no remote marker, and a bare country (Ashby's
  `Germany`, `Norway`, … 32 strings) parsed as kind `city`.

## Decision

The parser and two gazetteer variants are fixed, not the threshold (`sources/parsing/locations.py`, Task 6):

- **A comma qualifies a name only where the gazetteer confirms it:**
  - `<district>, <city>`: a German place of the district's name within 30 km (`DISTRICT_KM`) of a place of the
    city's name, and smaller than it. The 6a review found that without the population condition, lists of
    neighbouring cities (`Köln, Bonn`, `Berlin, Potsdam`, `Nürnberg, Fürth`, `Mannheim, Heidelberg`,
    `Düsseldorf, Neuss`) resolved as one city. With it they stay unresolved, and the numbers above are
    unchanged (fix round 1);
  - `<city>, <federal state>`: the German places of that name in that state, by GeoNames `admin1`, the state
    named in German or English;
  - `<city>, <state>, <country>`: the city in that state, or under its own name as a city-state, when the
    country is Germany. Abroad it is the city in that country, when the middle part is no German place,
    state, country or marker. Empty parts are dropped.
  - Lists stay unresolved and are never made foreign: `Berlin, Hamburg`, `Köln, Bonn`, `Berlin, Hamburg,
    Germany`, `Berlin, Hamburg, Austria`, `Deutschland, Österreich, Schweiz`, a head that is a country, state or
    marker. One
    Task 4 test row changes with this, deliberately: `Berlin, Berlin, Germany` now resolves to Berlin.
  - A qualifier country that contradicts every candidate keeps only the German ones: `Berlin, Austria` stays
    Berlin, and `Santa Cruz, Bolivia` no longer becomes Santa Cruz de Tenerife.
- **A trailing site word** (`Office`, `Büro`, `HQ`, `Headquarters`, `Campus`, `Hub`, `Site`) is dropped when
  what is left is a known place.
- **Markers and names:**
  - `Remote job` and `Remote jobs` are remote markers;
  - Germany's name in Dutch, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish, Czech, Scandinavian and Turkish counts as Germany
    (Arbeitnow writes `Hamburg, Hamburg, Duitsland`);
  - a part that is only a country's name gives kind `country_wide`, and it stays an unresolved location, so
    Task 4's "a country name is unresolved but positively foreign" holds.
- **Gazetteer variants:** `Bosnia` (BA), `Czech Republic` (CZ) and `Türkiye` (TR) were added to the
  hand-maintained `geo/data/country_names_de.tsv`. The three rows the build derives from it were added to
  `non_de_places.tsv` in the build's order, as a rebuild from the same dumps would write them. No new GeoNames
  download was made; the dumps change daily.

Limits that remain, none of them a V17 failure:

- A bare `Santa Cruz` (SmartRecruiters `location.city`; the record says Bolivia) resolves to the Spanish city;
  without a qualifier the string cannot tell.
- Ashby's `Georgia` is the US state (its `addressRegion`) but parses as the country Georgia. It is still
  positively not German, which is the outcome that matters.
- North American, Latin American and Turkish cities are not in the gazetteer (European cities over 100,000 only,
  §8.12), so they stay unresolved. Unresolved passes every filter.
