# V9: JSearch `search-v2` pages, buckets, cursor and rate limit

Status: **phases 1 and 2 recorded (2026-10-18); phase 3 (24 h, shared with V10) is scheduled
≥ 2026-10-19T20:50:21Z.**

## Assumption

JSearch bills 1 credit per page and nothing for a RapidAPI gateway 404 of the legacy route; the `date_posted`
buckets filter as named; a page's cursor can be reused hours later; 1 request/s is within the per-second limit
(spec §5.3.1, §22 V9). The spec expects the items in `data[]` with a `data.cursor`-style continuation; the
recording decides the paths.

## Method

`scripts/spikes/record_sources.py jsearch --phase 1` (budgeted, counted before each send; plan Task 6 step 4.1),
on 2026-10-18 from 20:50:09.807Z to 20:50:20.987Z, four requests with
`query="software engineer in Berlin, Germany"`, `country=de`, `num_pages=1`:

1. `GET /search` (the legacy route, `date_posted=week`) → `search_legacy_404`;
2. `GET /search-v2`, `date_posted=week` → `search_v2_page1`;
3. `GET /search-v2`, `date_posted=today` → `search_v2_today`;
4. `GET /search-v2`, `date_posted=week` and the cursor of (2), sent at once after (3) → `search_v2_page2`.

`record_sources.py jsearch --phase 2` (the recorder refuses it earlier than 2 h after phase 1), 2026-10-18
22:50:53Z, one request:

5. `GET /search-v2`, `date_posted=week` and the cursor of (2) again → `search_v2_page2_reused`. The cursor
   was 2.01 h old.

## Observed result

- **Legacy route:** 404 with `x-rapidapi-proxy-response: true` and `{"message": "Endpoint '/search' does not
  exist"}`; the gateway answers it, and it carries no `x-ratelimit-*` header.
- **Envelope of `search-v2`:** `{"status": "OK", "request_id": <uuid>, "parameters": {...}, "data": {"jobs":
  [...], "cursor": <string|null>}}`. The items are at **`data.jobs`** and the cursor at **`data.cursor`**. At the
  end of the results the key is present with **`null`** (`search_v2_today`), not absent. `parameters` echoes the
  request (`query`, `num_pages`, `date_posted`, `country`, the `cursor` when one was sent) and adds
  `language: "de"`, the default the spec relies on.
- **Billing, 1 credit per page:** `x-ratelimit-requests-remaining` was 198, 197, 196 after (2), (3), (4) of
  `x-ratelimit-requests-limit` 200 (the free-plans hard-limit counter fell in step: 499998, 499997, 499996 of
  500000). The empty `today` page cost 1 credit like a full one. `x-ratelimit-requests-reset` was 2,462,649 s at
  (2): the window runs to 2026-11-16T08:54:24Z and, with the nominal period of 2,675,242 s, began at
  2026-10-16T09:47:02Z. `R` after (2) is two below the limit, not one. That fits one earlier call in this
  window (its start is the minute of the plan's test clock, 2026-10-16 09:47) and an unbilled 404. The
  recording cannot prove which, since the 404 carries no counter. The same arithmetic on the later phases
  (one credit each) checks it again.
- **Buckets:** `week` returned 10 jobs on page 1, posted 68.8–140.8 h before `fetched_at`, and 9 on page 2,
  92.8–188.8 h. The timestamps are day-truncated (00:00 UTC of "vor N Tagen", N = 2–7), so the bucket is 7 days
  plus the truncated day (≤ 192 h). `today` (Sunday 20:50 UTC) returned 0 jobs and `cursor: null`. The 48-hour
  check of the plan is vacuous on an empty page; it is repeated on the phase-3 recordings.
- **Items:** `job_uid` is 24 characters on all 19 items; `job_id` is base64 of `<job_uid>:<276-byte blob>`;
  `job_city`, `job_state` and `job_country` are null on every item, so `job_location` (`Berlin • über
  <publisher>`, `Beliebiger Ort • über <publisher>`) is what is parsed; `job_posted_at` reads `vor N Tagen`
  (precision `day`). Page 2's 9 `job_uid`s are all new (none on page 1), and page 2 has its own cursor.
- **Per-second limit:** (3) was answered after 1.31 s, and (4) was sent 4 ms later; both 200. The two sends were
  1.32 s apart, the API's response time, so two requests within one second were not tested. Nothing observed
  contradicts 1 request/s.
- **Cursor reuse after 2 h (phase 2):** the 2.01-hour-old cursor of page 1 returned **200 with 9 jobs, 7 of
  them `job_uid`s of `search_v2_page2`** (none of page 1). The 2 others are new ads, and the order differs:
  the cursor still addresses page 2 of the same query, and the results have moved on a little. The page has
  its own cursor (a value different from page 2's). It cost **1 credit** (remaining 196 → 195); the
  window's reset fell by 7,237 s in the 2.01 h, so the window end is fixed (2026-11-16T08:54Z).
- **Cursor reuse after 24 h:** pending (phase 3).

## Decision

- `jsearch.ITEMS_PATH = ("data", "jobs")`, `jsearch.CURSOR_PATH = ("data", "cursor")`; the variant deriver ran with
  `--jsearch-items-path data.jobs --jsearch-cursor-path data.cursor`. **A `null` cursor means `END`**:
  `check_envelope` must accept the key with `null` (the recorded end of results), not only a missing key.
- 1 credit per page, an empty page included (§5.3.1 "2xx = billed" holds); the gateway 404 settles
  `not_billed` (`rapidapi_gateway_404`), consistent with the counters above.
- The rate limit const stays 1 request/s, concurrency 1.
- `CURSOR_REUSE_TTL` is at least `timedelta(hours=2)`: reuse was observed to work at 2 h (phase 2). It
  becomes `timedelta(hours=24)` if phase 3's request (7) returns a page overlapping `search_v2_page2`;
  otherwise it stays `timedelta(hours=2)`. Until phase 3 has run, Task 9 uses the observed `timedelta(hours=2)`,
  never an extrapolation.
