# V18: Adzuna `results_per_page`, `max_days_old` and `distance`

## Assumption

Adzuna silently caps `results_per_page=100` at 50; `max_days_old` keeps ads no older than the given days;
`distance` widens the search radius (spec §5.3.3, §22 V18). Acceptance (plan Task 6 step 4.5): (1) returns
exactly 50 results; every `created` of (2) is within 2 days of `fetched_at`; the `count` of (4) is at least the
`count` of (3).

## Method

`scripts/spikes/record_sources.py adzuna` on 2026-10-18, 20:53:06Z to 20:53:16Z (4 requests of the 5 allowed,
counted before each send), all `GET /v1/api/jobs/de/search/1` with `what=software engineer`, `where=Berlin`,
`sort_by=date`:

1. `results_per_page=100` → `search_p1_rpp100`;
2. `results_per_page=50`, `max_days_old=1` → `search_p1_days1`;
3. `results_per_page=50`, `distance=10` → `search_p1_distance10`;
4. `results_per_page=50`, `distance=50` → `search_p1_distance50`.

## Observed result

- **(1)** 50 results of `count` 516: the cap of 50 holds, so `count` > 50 continues to page 2.
- **(2)** `count` 10, 10 results, `created` 0.09 to 1.82 days before `fetched_at`: within 2 days. `max_days_old=1`
  admits ads up to almost two days old, so it counts calendar days, not 24-hour periods.
- **(3)** `count` 516, the same as (1), which sent no `distance`; **(4)** `count` 539 ≥ 516.
- No quota or rate-limit header in any response (`content-type`, `date` only): settlement cannot observe the
  quota, as §5.3.3 says.
- Every `description` is at most 500 characters (truncated: `partial`). `salary_is_predicted` is `"0"` on all
  50 results of (1); 8 of them carry `salary_min`/`salary_max`, so the recording holds stated salaries and no
  predicted one. `created` is ISO 8601 UTC. 49 of 50 results have `latitude`/`longitude`.
- `redirect_url` carries the app id in `utm_source` (the recorder replaced it before writing, the scrubber sets
  every `utm_*` to `scrubbed`). `adref` is a three-part token whose payload embeds the ad id. `id` is a
  10-digit string.

## Decision

- `results_per_page=50` stays the request value (100 buys nothing).
- `max_days_old = min(max_ad_age_days, ceil((now − last_covered_at) / 1 d) + 1)` (§5.5.3) stays: the `+ 1`
  covers the calendar-day semantics observed.
- `distance=<radius_km>` is sent as specified. The default radius matched 10 km for this query.
- `PAGE_CAP = 3` is unchanged. A recording with a predicted salary does not exist: the Task 10 contract
  assertion "some salary is `predicted`" cannot hold on `search_p1_rpp100` (raised in the Task 6 report).
