## New breakage in the fix diff

1. **Minor (pre-existing, widened): the lock session can be closed under a still-running heartbeat.** `main.py:258` `watchdog.stop(deadline.remaining(15))` joins for at most what is left of a 10 s teardown share; a heartbeat stuck on a silent TCP loss blocks up to `tcp_user_timeout` 20 s (`lock.py:32`), so the join can expire with the thread inside `conn.execute`. `_close_lock_session` then calls psycopg `Connection.close()`, which takes no lock and runs `pgconn.finish()` (`site-packages/psycopg/connection.py:187-201`) concurrently with libpq's in-flight `PQgetResult` — undefined behaviour, a possible crash at exit (signal instead of exit 1). The old 15 s join had the same gap against 20 s; the single deadline shortens the join. Fix: skip `close()` when `watchdog.is_alive()` after the join (the process exit drops the socket and releases the lock just the same), or `pgconn.cancel_safe()` first. Scenario needs budget exhaustion plus network loss during a stop; nothing in the fencing argument depends on it.

Nothing else: the fence introduces no new wait in the heartbeat path, no lock-order inversion, and no new unbounded wait (admissions wait only on a takeover, which is itself bounded by its lock_timeout; a frozen admission blocking a takeover is the accepted T13-b class, T21-i).

## Out-of-scope observations

- **A fenced delivery consumes its job.** `execute_run` returns `foreign_epoch` and `execute_operation` returns on `None` (`runs.py:650-651`, `operations.py:492-494`); the task body returns normally, so procrastinate marks the job done. A zombie that fetches a job in its ≤10 s residual life — including the `run_collect(id, a+1)` the takeover just deferred on the shared `collect` queue — leaves the work to `queued_runs`/`operations` (2 min) or `stale_runs` (3 min, one of `MAX_RESUMES` spent). Correctness holds; the `resumed` path behaved this way before the wave. A fenced admission could re-defer the same attempt/operation under its queueing lock (the `todo`-only unique index allows it while the current job is `doing`).
